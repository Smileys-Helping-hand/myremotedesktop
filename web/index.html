How it works

  1. Install it on both computers Download above, open the file, follow the installer. Nothing to sign up for.
  2. Open RemoteDesk on both Each one lists the other under My Devices, by name, as soon as they are on the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Press Join, then Allow Press Join on the computer you are sitting at. On the other one press Allow and share my screen. That is the whole thing.

Good to know

Nobody gets in without you

Out of the box, a connection has to be allowed on the computer being connected to. You can set a password instead, so your own machines connect without asking.

It updates itself

Open Get the App and press Check for updates. Computers on the same network can even update each other, with no internet.

Your screen goes straight there

The picture travels directly between the two computers over WebRTC. It is not stored or relayed through a server of ours.

Works from anywhere

Home, office, a friend's house — type the other computer's Desk ID and press Connect. No addresses, no port forwarding, nothing to set up on your router.
